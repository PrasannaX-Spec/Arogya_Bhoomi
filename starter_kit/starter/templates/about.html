About This Platform

Understanding the concept, methodology, and scope of the Traceable Recovery & Deployment Platform

The Problem

India generates massive quantities of expired pharmaceutical stock every year. In 2016 alone, the FDC ban led to over ₹400 crore worth of medicines being destroyed. Currently, this stock is incinerated or landfilled — there is no recovery pathway.

Meanwhile, Indian soils have severe micronutrient deficiencies: 49% are Zinc-deficient and over 33% are Iron-deficient (Naik et al., 2024). These are the same minerals present in common pharmaceutical products.

There is currently no system that connects these two sides — expired pharmaceutical stock and deficient agricultural regions — to enable safe, dosage-controlled reuse.

The Solution

This platform provides a 4-module pipeline that processes expired pharmaceutical batches from intake to deployment recommendation:

  1. Intake & Compliance — Verify the batch contains an approved single-compound medicine
  2. Geolocation — Resolve the batch's source location to geographic coordinates
  3. Soil-Deficiency Matching — Find the nearest region with documented deficiency in that compound's mineral
  4. Dosage Recommendation — Generate an ICAR-based application rate for the matched region

Dosage Methodology

Dosage recommendations use the ICAR General Recommended Dose (GRD) ±25% framework, a simplified version of the full STCR target-yield equations suitable for this prototype:

Severity Tier Adjustment Description
Low GRD + 25% Severely deficient soils need higher application
Medium GRD as-is Standard recommendation for moderately deficient soils
High GRD - 25% Less deficient soils need lower application
Ferrous Sulphate Exception: ICAR recommends a foliar spray route for iron correction (3-4 sprays of 1.0% FeSO₄ at weekly intervals), not soil application. This is handled as a dedicated branch in the dosage module.

Known Limitations

This is a hackathon prototype. The following limitations should be noted:

  • Soil deficiency data is static (ICAR 2012-2018), not a live government feed
  • Dosage uses simplified GRD ±25% rule, not full STCR equations
  • No real distributor/pharmacy has been onboarded — intake data is synthetic
  • Actual chemical extraction and logistics are out of scope
  • Only 3 compounds are currently supported
