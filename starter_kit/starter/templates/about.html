About Arogya Bhoomi

Traceable Micronutrient Recovery Platform connecting expired pharmaceutical stock with documented agricultural soil deficiencies.

THE PROBLEM

Expired pharmaceutical products can become difficult-to-track waste, creating environmental disposal challenges, while agricultural regions across India face widespread micronutrient soil deficiencies (such as Zinc and Iron deficiency).

OUR APPROACH

Arogya Bhoomi creates a traceable digital workflow connecting eligible pharmaceutical micronutrient compounds with documented soil deficiencies, transforming compliance-checked expired stock into verified agricultural recovery resources.

CURRENT APPROVED COMPOUNDS

  • Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO₄) — Single-ingredient approved compound
  • Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO₄) — Single-ingredient approved compound
  • Potassium Chloride (KCl) — Single-ingredient approved compound
* Multi-vitamin or combination products are strictly excluded from the approved whitelist.

WHAT AROGYA BHOOMI DOES

  1. Collects & records eligible expired pharmaceutical stock.
  2. Verifies approved compounds against the regulatory whitelist.
  3. Resolves source locations via geocoding service.
  4. Identifies documented soil deficiencies from authoritative state datasets.
  5. Matches suitable compounds with deficient geographic regions.
  6. Calculates application guidance using rule-based dosage models.
  7. Routes eligible recovery to certified downstream partner facilities.
  8. Records the recovery into an auditable ledger and generates reports.
  9. Supports soil re-testing and long-term application traceability.

TRACEABILITY ARCHITECTURE

Every stage of batch intake, compliance verification, matching, partner assignment, and soil deployment is recorded through a structured, hash-linked application audit trail. Each audit entry contains a canonical SHA-256 cryptographic link to the preceding event, ensuring tamper-evident data integrity across the platform lifecycle.

IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE: Arogya Bhoomi is a digital traceability, matching, and recovery coordination platform. Chemical extraction, purification, and re-formulation are performed by qualified, licensed downstream partner facilities and are outside the current software scope.
* Synthetic / illustrative demo data is used across initial records to demonstrate full platform capabilities for evaluation.
