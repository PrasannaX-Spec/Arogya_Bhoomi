Calculation Methodology & Dynamic Formulas

Transparent agronomic reference logic
1. Pharmaceutical Stock Diverted:

Sum of batch_qty_kg across all 45 intake records logged in the compliance database.

2. Elemental Nutrient Calculation:

Derived using stoichiometric active mineral ratios: Ferrous Sulphate (Fe: 20.1%), Zinc Sulphate (Zn: 21.0% / 33.0%), Potassium Chloride (K: 52.4%).

3. Addressable Agricultural Farmland (Hectares):

Calculated as (Zinc Qty ÷ 37.5 kg/ha) + (KCl Qty ÷ 40.0 kg/ha) + (FeSO4 Qty ÷ 12.5 kg/ha eq) using ICAR General Recommended Dosage (GRD) ±25% baseline rates.
